# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in this project to be safe, respectful, and welcoming
for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or technical
skill level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Discuss code and ideas without attacking people.
- Give actionable feedback and accept correction gracefully.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's credentials or personal
  information.
- Help keep issues and pull requests focused and useful.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized language and imagery.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, and repeated disruption.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately sharing working subscription credentials, HWIDs, or other
  secrets.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional community.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove comments, issues, pull requests,
and other contributions that violate this policy. Responses may range from a
private warning to a temporary or permanent ban, depending on severity and
repeated behavior.

## Reporting

For sensitive conduct reports, contact
[@Pavel2232](https://github.com/Pavel2232) through a private contact method
listed on the profile. Do not disclose another person's private information in
a public issue. Immediate threats can also be reported through GitHub's abuse
reporting tools.

This policy applies to project spaces and to public representation of the
project. It is informed by the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).

## По-русски

Общайтесь уважительно, обсуждайте код, а не людей, не публикуйте чужие данные и
секреты. Оскорбления, травля, угрозы и намеренное нарушение работы сообщества
не допускаются. Мейнтейнер может удалить такой контент и ограничить доступ к
проекту.
